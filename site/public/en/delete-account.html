Delete my account

You can delete your Kadans account at any time, with or without the app.

In the app

Settings → Delete my account, then confirm with your password. If you sign in with Google only, Kadans emails you a link to confirm instead.

Without the app

Open the account deletion page, enter your email address, and open the link we send you.

What happens

  • Your account closes at once: you are signed out everywhere.
  • Seven days later, it is erased with everything in it: tasks, focus history, budget. Sign in before then to keep it.
  • The copies in our nightly backups are gone 14 days after that.
  • A Google Play subscription stops renewing when the account is erased. To stop it sooner, cancel it in Google Play.
