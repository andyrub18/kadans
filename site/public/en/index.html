Three things, done well

Tasks that come back

Once, or on a rhythm: every morning, the 15th and the last day of the month, the last Friday. Kadans reminds you a little before, and again when it is time to start.

Focus, one cycle at a time

Give a task a Pomodoro cycle. Kadans keeps the time, asks before starting another cycle, and shows how long you really focused.

A budget in your currencies

Accounts in gourdes, US and Canadian dollars, euros, Dominican and Mexican pesos. Transfers with their exchange, limits per category, and the salary that books itself every month.

Your phone and your computer, in step

Mark a task done on one, and it is done on the other. Reminders ring on your phone even without internet, and the computer app waits quietly by the clock.
