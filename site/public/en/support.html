Common questions

My reminders don't ring on my Android phone.

In Kadans, open Settings → Reminders on this phone, and allow notifications and “Alarms & reminders”. Some phones also put apps to sleep: in your phone's battery settings, let Kadans run without restrictions.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and open the link we email you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Google Play: your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kadans. You keep Kadans until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete my account.

Is the computer app really free?

Yes. A subscription is only needed on phones.
