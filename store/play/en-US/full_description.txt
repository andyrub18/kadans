Kadans keeps your days in rhythm: your tasks, your focus and your money in one place, on your phone and on your computer.

TASKS THAT COME BACK
• Once, or on a rhythm: every morning, on weekdays, the 15th and the last day of the month, the last Friday…
• A reminder a little before, and another when it is time to start, even without internet.
• From the notification: start a focus session, mark it done, or put it off 10 minutes.
• A note on each time you did it, and the history of what you did.
• Share a text or a link from any app, and it becomes a task.

FOCUS, ONE CYCLE AT A TIME
• Give a task a Pomodoro cycle: focus, short breaks, a long break.
• Kadans keeps the time even when the app is closed, and asks before starting another cycle.
• See how long you really focused, day by day.

A BUDGET IN YOUR CURRENCIES
• Accounts in gourdes, US and Canadian dollars, euros, Dominican and Mexican pesos.
• Transfers with their exchange, and limits per category with an alert at 80% and at 100%.
• The salary and the rent come back by themselves, every month.
• Import a statement from your bank (CSV, OFX, QFX, CAMT.053): each line is booked once.

WHERE YOU ALREADY LOOK
• A widget on your home screen: today's tasks, done with one tap.
• Your tasks in your calendar (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook), and your phone's calendar beside your tasks.
• Do Not Disturb while you focus, if you turn it on.

ON EVERY SCREEN
• Your phone and your computer stay in step: done on one, done on the other.
• The computer app is free: kadansplanning.com

YOURS, AND ONLY YOURS
• No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.
• Two-factor authentication, and your sign-in sealed by your device.
• A copy of everything you put in whenever you want, and you can delete your account at any time.
